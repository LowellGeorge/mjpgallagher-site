Two Rivers

A trilogy of learning songs about how machines learn. Volume I follows deep learning, from McCulloch and Pitts to the Transformer. Volume II follows reinforcement learning, from Thorndike's cat to the dopamine neuron. Volume III is where the two rivers meet: DeepMind, AlphaGo, RLHF, and how the Chinese labs do more with less. With an overture, a finale and a hidden track, that makes thirty-three songs, each with its own video. Watch and listen →
